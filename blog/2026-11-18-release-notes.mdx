## Overview

**The `2026-11-18.0` release of Overture data and `v2.0.0` of the Overture schema are now available.** Buildings grew by 10.3 million features, led by a refreshed Esri Community Maps extract that added 5.3 million buildings. Places grew 2.1% to 90.3 million features.

You can access the datasets, changelog, GERS registry, bridge files, and PMTiles on AWS and Azure at the paths listed below.


## Getting the data and release artifacts

You can access this month's data and release artifacts by following the process outlined in our [docs](/getting-data). We encourage you to ask questions and provide feedback on the Overture Maps [Discussion forum on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/OvertureMaps/discussions). You can also file issues and report bugs in our [data](https://github.com/OvertureMaps/data/issues) and [schema](https://github.com/OvertureMaps/schema/issues) repositories. If you have a suggestion for a new dataset or if you have data you'd like to contribute to Overture, you can email us at community@overturemaps.org. We'd love to hear from you.

## Theme-specific updates

:::info
The base, buildings, divisions, places, and transportation themes are in GA. The addresses theme is in alpha.
:::

### Addresses

- Address count went from 475,263,892 to 475,239,461, a decrease of 0.01%.
- Riverside County, California (`us/ca/riverside`) fell from 942,294 to 811,215 addresses, a decrease of 13.91%. The upstream OpenAddresses source removed records without a house number; Riverside addresses with a house number grew slightly, from 809,103 to 811,127.
- Latvian addresses no longer carry the postcode inside `address_levels`; the postcode stays in `postcode`. All 550,972 Latvian addresses received new GERS IDs as a result. Latvian addresses now credit Valsts zemes dienests (the State Land Service), which produces the data.
- Empty `address_levels` arrays are now null, and the affected addresses keep their GERS IDs.
- GERS churn is 0.99%, with 2,331,669 addresses added and 2,356,100 removed.
- Core field coverage is stable: `street` 98.52% to 98.52%, `number` 92.80% to 92.83%, `postcode` 78.70% to 78.69%, and `address_levels_2` 78.74% to 78.73%.

### Places

Places grew 2.12% this release, led by BrightQuery and Meta, and Overture now normalizes address countries, regions, and phone numbers for every source.

- Place count grew from 88,418,121 to 90,295,748, an increase of 2.12%.
- BrightQuery places grew from 15,771,907 to 16,774,347, an increase of 6.36%. Meta places grew from 60,299,571 to 61,265,630, an increase of 1.60%. Uberall places grew from 424,600 to 491,470, an increase of 15.75%.
- Overture now normalizes three place properties for every source. `addresses[].country` is an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code, and `addresses[].region` is an ISO 3166-2 code; a region that can't be matched to a valid code is set to null. `phones` are in E.164 format, and numbers that can't be parsed are dropped. As a result, 57,292,153 places (64.80%) carry changed data, mostly in `addresses` and `phones`, and keep their GERS IDs.
- PinMeTo records now use a fixed confidence of 0.9 instead of the feed's open or closed flag. Where PinMeTo overlaps other sources, another source's record now more often supplies the place's primary attributes, so places with PinMeTo as the primary source fell from 170,156 to 150,879, a decrease of 11.33%.
- Places marked `open` grew 5.74%, from 27,103,902 to 28,660,871. Places marked `permanently_closed` fell 4.89%, from 1,666,495 to 1,585,060, and places marked `temporarily_closed` grew from 3,496 to 4,653.
- GERS churn is 6.55%, with 3,835,684 places added and 1,958,051 removed.
