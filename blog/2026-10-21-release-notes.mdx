## Overview

**The `2026-10-21.0` release of Overture data and `v2.0.0` of the Overture schema are now available.** Places grew 8.5% to 88.4 million features, as BrightQuery coverage expanded across Western Europe and three new places sources were added: Uberall, Birdeye, and OpenTravel.

You can access the datasets, changelog, GERS registry, bridge files, and PMTiles on AWS and Azure at the paths listed below.


## Getting the data and release artifacts

You can access this month's data and release artifacts by following the process outlined in our [docs](/getting-data). We encourage you to ask questions and provide feedback on the Overture Maps [Discussion forum on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/OvertureMaps/discussions). You can also file issues and report bugs in our [data](https://github.com/OvertureMaps/data/issues) and [schema](https://github.com/OvertureMaps/schema/issues) repositories. If you have a suggestion for a new dataset or if you have data you'd like to contribute to Overture, you can email us at community@overturemaps.org. We'd love to hear from you.

## Theme-specific updates

:::info
The base, buildings, divisions, places, and transportation themes are in GA. The addresses theme is in alpha.
:::

### Addresses

- Address count grew from 474,186,531 to 475,263,892, an increase of 0.23%.
- Replaced the countrywide dataset for Uruguay (`UY`) with 19 regional datasets, one per department, sourced from Direcciones Geográficas del Uruguay. Uruguay now has 1,052,295 addresses, down from 1,059,027, and `address_levels` coverage in Uruguay rose from 85.76% to 100%.
- Refreshed data from the US National Address Database; gained 935,021 addresses, an increase of 1.11%. New Caledonia (`NC`) grew from 41,536 to 51,702 addresses, an increase of 24.48%.
- Core field coverage is stable: `street` 98.52% to 98.52%, `number` 92.78% to 92.80%, `postcode` 78.87% to 78.70%, and `address_levels_2` 76.11% to 78.74%.

### Places

Places grew 8.5% this release, driven by the expansion of BrightQuery coverage across Western Europe and the addition of three new sources.

- Place count grew from 81,455,423 to 88,418,121, an increase of 8.55%.
- BrightQuery places grew from 10,255,071 to 15,771,907, an increase of 53.8%; the largest additions are France (1,949,361), the United Kingdom (1,447,574), and Spain (1,225,103).
- Added three new sources: Uberall (424,600 places across 217 countries, led by Germany, the United States, and France), Birdeye (11,855 places, mostly in the United States), and OpenTravel (2,698 Hilton properties).
- In the 10 countries BrightQuery now covers (the United Kingdom, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Sweden, Norway, and Finland), 1,861,523 existing places now carry BrightQuery data in place of their previous Meta, Foursquare, or Microsoft record, and keep their GERS IDs. As a result, primary source counts for those providers fell in those countries.
- Operating status coverage improved, from 23.74% to 32.54% of places, driven mostly by the BrightQuery expansion. Places marked `open` grew 47.6%, from 18,361,914 to 27,103,902, with BrightQuery places accounting for 4,962,192 of the increase. Places marked `permanently_closed` grew 70.2%, from 978,895 to 1,666,495, with BrightQuery places accounting for 554,597 of the increase. Places marked `temporarily_closed` grew from 29 to 3,496. Operating status is now chosen by majority vote across a place's contributing sources, and `sources` records which source supplied it under the `/properties/operating_status` property.
- GERS churn is 11.92%, with 8,335,526 places added and 1,372,826 removed, driven by the BrightQuery expansion and removals.
